Clear Pricing

Clear Pricing Before Any Work Starts

Good water heater service starts with knowing exactly what you're dealing with. Hattiesburg Water Heater offers honest, upfront pricing on every job, covering everything from a failed heating element to a full tank replacement. Every visit begins with a thorough assessment of your unit, including its age, capacity, fuel type, venting, and the condition of the tank and valves, followed by a plain-English recommendation with the price spelled out before any work starts.

The trouble with water heater failures is that many of them develop inside the tank where you can't see them. Minerals settle into a crust on the tank floor, the anode rod wears down a little at a time, and when it's gone, the tank itself starts to corrode. When hot water comes out rusty, the tank rumbles, or a small drip appears at the base, have it checked before a repair turns into a replacement.

We also help homeowners plan ahead. Most tank water heaters last roughly 8 to 12 years, and a planned water heater replacement is nearly always less expensive and less stressful than an emergency swap after the tank has already leaked. Our technicians know how local water conditions wear on tanks over time, so we can tell you honestly whether your heater has years left or is living on borrowed time.

Maintenance Plans

Tank Flushing and Tune-Ups That Pay Off

A maintenance visit from Hattiesburg Water Heater is far more than opening the drain valve. Our technician flushes the sediment, pulls and inspects the anode rod, lifts and tests the relief valve, checks the burner, pilot, and venting on gas heaters, tests the elements and thermostats on electric units, and confirms a safe water temperature. Minor faults get caught while they're still cheap to fix, and your water heater keeps running at full efficiency.

No two maintenance plans look exactly alike. A tank in its later years gets a closer look than a brand-new heater, a tankless unit on hard water needs regular descaling to keep its heat exchanger working, and a household of five works the tank far harder than a couple does. We set up each visit around your equipment and water, so every service keeps the unit efficient and protects the equipment you've already paid for.

Homeowners are often surprised how much a consistent maintenance schedule helps. A clean tank heats water faster and more quietly, a healthy anode rod protects the steel from corrosion, and a working relief valve is ready to vent pressure if something goes wrong. Most families find that annual service costs far less than replacing a water heater even a few years early.

Right-Sized Hot Water

A Water Heater Sized for Your Actual Household

An undersized water heater runs out halfway through the second shower, while an oversized one wastes energy keeping extra gallons hot around the clock. Hattiesburg Water Heater sizes every replacement and new installation to your actual household. For tank models, that means matching the first-hour rating to your morning rush, not just copying the gallon size on the old tank. For tankless models, it means sizing the gallons per minute to the fixtures you run at once and the temperature of your incoming water.

Choosing a new water heater is about more than tank size. We'll compare tankless units against a traditional tank, and gas against electric or hybrid, walking through operating costs, venting and gas line requirements, electrical capacity, and the installation itself. Tankless works well for many households, though it often requires a bigger gas line and new venting, while a well-sized tank is often the simpler, lower-cost option. You choose what fits your home and budget.

Repairs We Handle

Hot Water Problems We Solve Every Week

A cold tap where hot water should be, a burner that refuses to light, a tank full of sediment, a slow drip from the relief valve: every one of these problems has its own cause and calls for its own repair. Hattiesburg Water Heater diagnoses the exact fault before replacing anything, whether it's an electric tank with a burned-out element, a gas heater with a failing thermocouple, a broken dip tube sending cold water straight to the tap, or a tankless unit throwing error codes.

Not every water heater problem is equally urgent. A bad element just means lukewarm showers. A relief valve that discharges, on the other hand, is a safety device doing its job and telling you something is wrong, and water pooling under the tank can quietly ruin flooring before anyone notices. Smell gas? Leave the house and call your gas utility before anything else. Our technicians carry the parts to swap elements, thermostats, thermocouples, gas valves, and relief valves, add a thermal expansion tank when pressure is the culprit, and handle leak repair at the drain valve or fittings.
